01About

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electronics, Communication and Information Engineering from Thapathali Engineering Campus, Tribhuvan University. I currently work at Smart Data Solutions, a US-based company, on medical image classification. Before that, I spent over two years at NsDevil, a Korean EdTech company, where I started as an AI Research Intern and went on to build LLM-based scoring, speech evaluation and retrieval systems used in production.

Outside work, I run two independent AI safety research projects, CHANAKYA and SafetyBox, both about making sure AI systems only do what they are allowed to do. My interests are AI safety, AI governance, agentic AI, NeuroAI and AI in healthcare. In the long term, I plan to contribute to AI safety and governance.

05Ongoing AI Safety Research

CHANAKYA

Web2 vulnerability detection and authorization research · Ongoing
  • A research system for finding IDOR/BOLA, missing authorization and other broken access control failures in source code.
  • Combines deterministic authorization evidence with bounded AI investigation, so every conclusion traces back to concrete code and control flow, not a guess.
  • Current focus on Java and Spring authorization patterns, with techniques meant to generalize to other Web2 stacks.

SafetyBox

Autonomous-agent safety and authorization · Ongoing
  • Studies how autonomous agents can behave in unsafe or "rogue" ways when probabilistic reasoning directly controls privileged actions.
  • Explores task-scoped authorization, runtime mediation and capability limits, so reasoning stays flexible while final authority stays deterministic.
  • Builds adversarial and counterfactual cases to measure false-allow and false-deny behavior.
