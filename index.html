About

Building AI systems, and checking that they behave.

I am an AI/ML engineer from Nepal with over two years of experience building and evaluating production AI systems. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electronics, Communication and Information Engineering from Thapathali Engineering Campus, Tribhuvan University.

I currently work at Smart Data Solutions on medical image classification. In my own time I run two AI safety research projects, CHANAKYA and SafetyBox. In the long term, I plan to contribute to AI safety and governance.

Ongoing AI safety research

CHANAKYA

Authorization and vulnerability research · Ongoing

Finds IDOR/BOLA, missing authorization and other broken access control in source code. It pairs deterministic authorization evidence with bounded AI investigation, so every finding traces back to real code and control flow. Current focus is Java and Spring.

SafetyBox

Autonomous-agent safety · Ongoing

Studies how agents go "rogue" when probabilistic reasoning directly controls privileged actions. Reasoning stays flexible, but task-scoped authorization and runtime checks keep the final say deterministic. Includes adversarial cases for false-allow and false-deny behavior.
